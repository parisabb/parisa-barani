About

Space, stories and how we live together.

Parisa Barani is an interior architect with a Bachelor’s degree in Architecture and a Master’s degree in Spatial Design from Konstfack, completed in 2024. She works as a freelance designer on renovation, residential and retail interior projects.

Based

Stockholm, Sweden

Focus

Spatial Design, Interior Architecture, Exhibition Design and Production

Interested in space as a form of storytelling, she considers how environments can create context, communicate ideas and shape encounters between people. Her practice brings together research, concept development, material exploration and production, guided by an interest in cultural exchange, participation and the capacity of shared spaces to hold many voices.
