About

Space, stories and how we live together.

Parisa Barani is an interior architect and spatial designer based in Stockholm. Her work moves between interiors, exhibitions, public space and artistic research.

Based

Stockholm, Sweden

Focus

Spatial Design, Interior Architecture, Exhibition Design and Production

Independent practice

Studio Baran

Parisa is interested in how spaces shape the way people feel, meet and move. Her work brings together research, material and storytelling, moving between professional collaborations and self-initiated projects. The aim is simple: to create thoughtful, inclusive places with room for people, ideas and everyday life.
