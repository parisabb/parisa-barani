A multidisciplinary design practice.

Parisa Barani is a spatial designer and artist based in Stockholm. Her practice moves between interiors, public space, installations and artistic research.

Based

Independent practice

Studio Baran

Through material, atmosphere and storytelling, she creates spaces and objects that encourage connection, reflection and new ways of being together. Her work brings together professional design experience with self-initiated research, exploring how spatial design can support more inclusive and meaningful everyday environments.
