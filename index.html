About

Parisa Barani is a Stockholm-based interior architect and spatial designer. Her work begins with close attention to place: its rhythms, histories and the ways people gather within it.

Across interiors, exhibitions and spatial interventions, she uses colour, form and material to shape distinct atmospheres. Each project becomes a framework for encounter - a place to move through, interpret and share.

With a Master’s degree in Spatial Design from Konstfack University and a Bachelor’s degree in Architecture, she works between design practice and artistic inquiry. Her work is grounded in making, collaboration and the belief that shared environments can hold many voices.
